# Domain 12 — Off-Page SEO

## Language and Canonical Versions

English is the sole publication language of SEO-Playbook. Each published topic has one canonical English document; parallel language editions are not maintained.

## Domain Purpose

Domain 12 explains how websites establish, earn, evaluate, and manage their presence and relationships beyond their own properties, with particular attention to external links, earned media, backlink evaluation, and off-site reputation.

Its canonical focus is **practitioner-side external presence and reference management**. It covers why external references occur, how legitimate link opportunities are identified and evaluated, how outreach and digital PR should be conducted, how backlink evidence should be interpreted, and how link-related policy risks should be addressed.

Off-page SEO is not synonymous with link quantity, a third-party authority score, or a guaranteed ranking mechanism. External activity can support referral, awareness, relationships, and reputation even when no ranking effect is established. Claims about search-engine behavior must remain bounded by the evidence.

## Scope and Ownership

### What Domain 12 owns

- Foundations and interpretation of off-page SEO and external references.
- Link earning and ethical external-link acquisition principles.
- Link prospecting, opportunity qualification, outreach, and relationship development.
- Digital PR, media relations, and earned-media activity where it intersects with external visibility.
- Practitioner-side backlink profile analysis and link evaluation.
- Link-spam policy interpretation, risk assessment, and remediation workflows.
- General brand mentions, off-site visibility, and reputation interpretation.

### Canonical Boundary Rules

1. **Practitioner activity is not provider mechanism.** Domain 12 owns what site owners and practitioners do; Domain 03 owns search-engine link-analysis and spam systems.
2. **External links are not internal links.** Domain 11 owns links between pages within a website; Domain 12 owns references originating outside it.
3. **A mention is not automatically a backlink or ranking factor.** Unlinked mentions, editorial citations, reviews, and hyperlinks are distinct observations.
4. **Content production is not external promotion.** Domain 08 owns creating and maintaining content; Domain 12 owns external distribution, reference opportunities, and earned-media practice.
5. **Local reputation has a specialist home.** Local listings, citations, and review operations belong primarily to Domain 13.
6. **Backlink evaluation is not general SEO analytics.** Domain 12 owns link-specific dataset interpretation; Domain 14 owns broader organic performance measurement.
7. **Vendor metrics are not search-engine scores.** Third-party authority metrics must be identified as provider-defined models, with their data and methodological limitations.
8. **No ranking guarantees.** Link acquisition, media coverage, and brand mentions must not be presented as guaranteed ranking improvements.
9. **Policy compliance is contextual.** Paid and sponsored links, user-generated links, and other commercial relationships must be interpreted under current platform guidance and applicable law.

## Cross-Domain Relationships

- **01.5 → Domain 12:** the SEO discipline overview introduces Off-Page SEO; Domain 12 supplies the specialist knowledge.
- **03.5 → Domain 12:** search-engine link-analysis concepts provide context; Domain 12 evaluates practitioner-side external references without reproducing PageRank mechanisms.
- **03.7 → Domain 12:** provider-side spam detection and treatment inform practitioner policy compliance and remediation.
- **08 → Domain 12:** useful content assets may create legitimate reasons for external citation; Domain 08 owns asset creation and Domain 12 owns external promotion and reference acquisition.
- **09 → Domain 12:** semantic entity context may help interpret mentions; Domain 09 owns semantic modeling while Domain 12 owns monitoring and reputation practice.
- **11 ↔ Domain 12:** both concern hyperlinks but have distinct scopes: internal website relationships versus external references.
- **Domain 12 → Domain 13:** local listings, citations, and review operations are handed off to the local-search specialist domain.
- **Domain 12 → Domain 14:** link-specific evidence and campaign outcomes can feed broader measurement; Domain 14 owns general SEO analytics.
- **Domain 12 → Domain 17:** novel tactics without adequate evidence remain experimental rather than being presented as established guidance.

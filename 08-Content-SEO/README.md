# Domain 08 — Content SEO

## Domain Purpose

Domain 08 documents the editorial discipline behind planning, developing, producing, reviewing, improving, and maintaining website content.

Its canonical focus is the **substance and editorial lifecycle of content**: why a content asset should exist, what it needs to communicate, how its information is developed, how it is produced and reviewed, and how its usefulness and accuracy are maintained over time.

Content SEO is not a synonym for keyword placement, HTML optimization, semantic architecture, or search-engine content-quality systems. It is the work of making content purposeful, useful, accurate, coherent, and responsibly maintained. Search visibility may be one objective, but it does not replace the audience's actual task or justify unsupported claims.

This domain owns editorial decisions and workflows. It does not claim ownership of every technical, research, or distribution activity that touches a content asset.

## Scope and Ownership

### What Domain 08 owns

- Content SEO foundations, principles, and editorial responsibilities.
- Content strategy and editorial governance.
- Editorial planning and content portfolio decisions.
- Translating approved research handoffs into editorial plans.
- Content briefs, editorial specifications, and acceptance criteria.
- Development of information outlines, arguments, explanations, and substantive content flow.
- Editorial content creation and production workflows across relevant formats.
- Source verification, fact-checking, editorial review, and publication readiness.
- Original editorial contribution, including responsibly sourced research, analysis, examples, and first-hand experience where relevant.
- Substantive content optimization and revision.
- Editorial maintenance, content updates, and lifecycle decisions.
- Content inventory and audits focused on editorial substance and usefulness.

### Canonical Boundary Rules

1. **Research handoff is the starting input, not a second research project.** Domain 05 owns keyword discovery, intent analysis, clustering, prioritization, and keyword-to-page mapping. Domain 08 consumes those outputs and makes editorial decisions.
2. **Editorial quality is not a ranking-system model.** Domain 03 owns search-engine quality and ranking systems. Domain 08 owns editorial standards, verification, review, and content usefulness. Do not invent an E-E-A-T score, helpfulness score, originality score, or ranking weight.
3. **Content substance is not page markup.** Domain 08 owns the meaning, completeness, argument, and usefulness of the content. Domain 07 owns title elements, meta descriptions, HTML heading hierarchy, image alt text, semantic HTML, and on-page implementation.
4. **Editorial outlines are not heading-element specifications.** Domain 08 may organize ideas and sections to develop a coherent explanation. Domain 07 owns the technical and semantic use of HTML heading elements.
5. **Content architecture is not semantic architecture.** Domain 09 owns entity modeling, semantic relationships, topical coverage frameworks, and semantic content architecture. Domain 08 uses relevant outputs without rebuilding those methods.
6. **Content updates are not freshness ranking systems.** Domain 08 decides whether information needs editorial maintenance and performs or commissions the content revision. Domain 03 owns search-engine freshness and temporal ranking mechanisms.
7. **Content audits are not on-page audits or performance reports.** Domain 08 evaluates editorial substance and lifecycle condition; Domain 07 evaluates page-level elements; Domain 14 measures organic performance and outcomes.
8. **Editorial relationships are not internal-link architecture.** Domain 08 may note where a reader needs a related resource. Domain 11 owns internal-link strategy, architecture, and implementation.
9. **Original contribution requires substance and provenance.** Domain 08 may cover original research, analysis, examples, and first-hand experience as editorial methods. It must not imply that adding anecdotes, author bios, or superficial novelty guarantees rankings.
10. **Formats are production contexts, not separate ownership silos.** Articles, guides, product content, video, visual assets, and other formats should be addressed where they affect planning or production—not split into standalone topics without a distinct knowledge need.
11. **Performance evidence informs editorial decisions but does not prove causation by itself.** Domain 14 owns measurement and analysis. Domain 08 must distinguish observed performance from editorial diagnosis and proposed explanations.
12. **Experimental claims stay experimental.** A tactic does not become canonical guidance because it worked once or sounds plausible. Follow Domain 17's evidence and maturity requirements.

## Cross-Domain Integration

- **Domain 01 → 08:** Domain 01 introduces Content SEO as one SEO discipline; Domain 08 provides the detailed editorial knowledge.
- **Domain 03 → 08:** Search quality, helpfulness, originality, and freshness systems provide search-system context. Domain 08 owns editorial execution, not ranking-system explanations.
- **Domain 05 → 08:** Validated keyword research, intent evidence, prioritization, and keyword-to-page mapping provide inputs for content planning and briefs.
- **Domain 06 ↔ 08:** SERP and search-feature observations may inform editorial context, but search-result presentation remains Domain 06.
- **Domain 07 ↔ 08:** Editorial substance and page-level elements must be consistent. Domain 08 owns content meaning and editorial quality; Domain 07 owns page elements, markup, and on-page implementation.
- **Domain 08 ↔ Domain 09:** Semantic architecture may inform content planning and development. Domain 09 owns the semantic models and architecture; Domain 08 owns editorial realization.
- **Domain 08 ↔ Domain 10:** Editorial requirements and assets feed technical implementation; technical delivery and search-engine access remain Domain 10.
- **Domain 08 ↔ Domain 11:** Editorial context may identify useful relationships between resources; internal-link architecture and implementation remain Domain 11.
- **Domain 08 ↔ Domain 12:** Domain 08 creates content assets; off-site promotion, digital PR, and link acquisition remain Domain 12.
- **Domain 08 ↔ Domain 13:** Editorial content may need market, language, and local adaptation; international/local targeting methodology remains Domain 13.
- **Domain 14 → 08:** Performance analysis can inform editorial review. Domain 14 owns measurement, attribution, and reporting; Domain 08 owns resulting editorial decisions.
- **Domain 17 ↔ 08:** Experiments can generate hypotheses for editorial practice. They remain experimental until evidence and review justify canonical adoption.

## Research and Documentation Principles

- Start from the existing canonical documentation before researching a new topic. Extend or cross-link existing explanations rather than rewriting them.
- For Google Search claims, consult the relevant current Google Search Central documentation first, while keeping Google's published guidance distinct from independent editorial research.
- Use primary research, original datasets, standards, and first-hand evidence where the claim requires them. Do not use generic SEO commentary as a substitute for evidence.
- Distinguish editorial standards, accessibility or usability rationale, search-engine documentation, practitioner convention, interpretation, and hypothesis.
- Do not manufacture universal content length, keyword density, update frequency, publishing cadence, originality score, or editorial score thresholds.
- Treat content formats as context-dependent production choices, not ranking shortcuts.
- Document source provenance, uncertainty, material revisions, and editorial accountability.
- Keep editorial recommendations separate from technical implementation and performance attribution.
- Follow the mandatory claim-level citation policy in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Every substantive topic document must include inline citations and a References section.
